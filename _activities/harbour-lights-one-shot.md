Something strange is flickering beneath Sydney Harbour. Gather a crew, choose a ready-made character and follow the clues before the lights disappear.

## Joining a session

Each session is designed for first-time and experienced roleplayers. Characters, dice and rules guidance are provided. Final booking details will be added with the full schedule.
