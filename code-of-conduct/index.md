All Play Day is for everyone. We want every player, maker, guest and staff member to feel welcome, comfortable and safe.

## Play kindly

Respect personal boundaries, ask before taking photos, use the names and pronouns people share, and leave room at the table. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation and unwanted physical contact are not welcome.

## Need support?

Please speak with an All Play Day or Fortress staff member at any time, or use our [contact form]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}). We will listen, respond with care and take appropriate action.
